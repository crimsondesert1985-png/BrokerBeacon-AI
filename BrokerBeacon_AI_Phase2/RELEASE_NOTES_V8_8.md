# BrokerBeacon AI v8.8 — Loan Guidelines Library

## Added
- Dedicated Loan Guidelines navigation section.
- Searchable program summaries for Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA, VA, and USDA loans.
- Quick-reference cards for eligibility, occupancy/property, financing, insurance/fees, underwriting focus, and AE discovery questions.
- Direct links to each agency's controlling guide and update resources.
- Prominent compliance language distinguishing quick-reference guidance from agency rules, AUS findings, lender overlays, and underwriting decisions.

## Design choice
Mutable figures such as annual loan limits, county income limits, fee percentages, and lender overlays are not hard-coded as permanent facts. Users are directed to official live sources.
