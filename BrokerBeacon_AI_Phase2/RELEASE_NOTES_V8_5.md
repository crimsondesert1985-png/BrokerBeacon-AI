# BrokerBeacon AI v8.5 — Ash Sales Coach

## Added
- New Ash Sales Coach workspace and navigation item.
- Explainable daily coaching queue based on stored account data.
- Relationship-health assessment, response-likelihood heuristic, why-today reasoning, opportunity angle, suggested call opening, and likely objection.
- Filters for Call Today, At Risk, and High Response accounts.
- One-click Prepare This Account action using the existing Start My Day workflow.
- Direct call, email, and account-intelligence actions.

## Accuracy and safety
Response likelihood is labeled as a modeled heuristic. It uses BrokerBeacon activity and opportunity data and is not a guarantee of production or response.
